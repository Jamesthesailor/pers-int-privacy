Pers-Int Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We collect almost nothing. Here's exactly what happens with your data:

Your dossiers stay on your device

Names, emails, usernames, search results — all stored locally in the app on your phone. We never see them, never store them on our servers, never transmit them anywhere.

Search queries

When you run a username or email check, your device contacts public websites directly. We do not route these queries through our servers and we do not log them.

Tokens

Token purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We don't see or store your payment information.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs. No advertising SDKs. No crash reporting that includes personal data. No ads of any kind — ever.

What we cannot do

We cannot share what we do not have. We cannot sell what we never collect. We cannot leak what never leaves your phone.

Data deletion

Since all your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. There is nothing on our servers to delete because we never receive your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the app's About section.
